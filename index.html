01 Capabilities

Services

Practical, outcomes-first support—delivered directly by the specialist who does the work. No handoffs, no middle layer.

Market Research & Targeting

Identify who buys what, where, and when—by agency, NAICS and PSC code, set-aside lane, and contract vehicle—so your effort goes where your PWin is highest.

SAM.gov & Set-Aside Readiness

Get registered right the first time—UEI, CAGE code, NAICS and PSC—and map your path to the set-aside lanes you may qualify for: 8(a), HUBZone, SDVOSB, and WOSB.

Capture Management

Qualify the pipeline, shape opportunities early, build teaming relationships, and make disciplined bid/no-bid calls—capture that raises PWin, not just activity.

Proposal Support

Compliant, compelling RFP and RFQ responses—clean compliance matrices, sharp past-performance narratives, and pricing built to win.

02 Method

Our Process

Find → Position → Win

From confusion to contract—handled directly, start to finish.

1. Find Opportunities

Targeted research across SAM.gov Contract Opportunities—historical spend, contract vehicles, and the Sources Sought and RFIs that signal what’s coming.

2. Position to Win

Capture actions, teaming, and early engagement with the buying office—plus bid/no-bid rigor, so you commit resources only where PWin justifies it.

3. Win & Deliver

Disciplined RFP and RFQ execution, a clean handoff, and reporting set up so delivery—and your CPARS record—starts strong.

05 Who You're Working With

About

Most small businesses entering the U.S. Federal market hit the same walls: registration and compliance feel overwhelming, proposals miss the details evaluators score on, and opportunities slip away because targeting and timing aren’t precise. That’s where we come in.

With 15+ years across mechanical engineering, heavy industry, and military operations, I’ve built a career on making complex systems work under pressure. I now bring that same discipline to federal contracting—as an advisory partner helping small businesses close the gaps that keep them from winning. We’re not a bidder and we don’t claim your set-aside status; you stay the prime, and we help you compete and win.

What We Do for You

  • Targeted market research — pinpoint who buys what, where, and when, across agencies, NAICS and PSC codes, set-aside lanes, and contract vehicles.
  • Capture focused on highest PWin — prioritize the opportunities that fit your capabilities, timelines, and past performance, and shape them early.
  • Relationships with the buying office — engage COs, CORs, and PMs before the solicitation, build trust, and set the stage for repeat work.

Where small businesses fall short, we turn weak spots into winning strategy— so you don’t just compete, you win. We don’t bid; we help you win.
